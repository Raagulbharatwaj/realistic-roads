## Description (Steam formatting)

```
[h1]Realistic Roads (BETA)[/h1]
[b]Plan your roads and add layers to your city.[/b]

Draw a whole route in one go, see it planned from the side, choose how it crosses everything in its way, then build it. Flyovers over busy streets, roads sunk under rail lines, tunnels under rivers, double-decker roads and metro running one level under the street: your city grows up and down, not just out.

[b]This is a beta.[/b] Save your city before building with it, and please report problems (see below).

[h2]Plan your roads[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Draw the whole route at once:[/b] long, smooth curves with few nodes, or straight pieces with rounded corners, angle and length snapping, and typed lengths and turns.
[*][b]See it before you build it:[/b] when you finish drawing, the road stays as a plan. Drag its points, check the side panel, and only then build. Ctrl+Z undoes the whole road.
[*][b]Every crossing is listed and numbered[/b] in order along the road, on the map and in the panel, each with its choices: level junction, bridge, overpass, underpass or tunnel.
[*][b]Side view:[/b] the road against the ground and water. Drag a point's height up or down, or make a stretch (or the whole road) Elevated or Tunnel; the road ramps to it at its grade.
[*][b]Realistic grades and clearances:[/b] 4 % ramps for roads, gentle slopes for lowered rail, 9 m under flyovers, extra room over rail wires, and 20 m over ship routes.
[/list]

[h2]Add layers to your city[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Flyovers and bridges[/b] over roads and rail, on long spans with few pillars.
[*][b]Overpasses and underpasses:[/b] keep your road at ground level and lower the other one, or dip under an elevated road.
[*][b]Tunnels[/b] under roads and through hills.
[*][b]Water:[/b] bridges over rivers and sea, raised for ship routes, or a tunnel under the water bed.
[*][b]Double-decker roads:[/b] build a road on top of an existing one, on columns in its median or frames across it, clear of its street lights.
[*][b]Underground deck:[/b] run a road or metro line one level under an existing street, like most real cities.
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Pick a road, rail or metro track in the game's menus, then click the [b]RR[/b] button (or press [b]Ctrl+Shift+C[/b]).
[*]Click to place points along the route ([b]Tab[/b]: curved or straight pieces; the Deck and Under buttons build over or under the roads below).
[*]Double-click or press [b]Enter[/b] to finish drawing; check the plan in the panel and pick each crossing.
[*]Press [b]Enter[/b] or the check mark to build.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] (subscribe to it).
[/list]

[h2]Known limits (beta)[/h2]
[list]
[*]A road that lowers a rail line very close to a junction may not fit at the rail's gentle grade: cross further from the junction.
[*]Buildings in the way are not removed: bulldoze them first (the panel warns you).
[*]Not tested with every road and track mod; please report the ones that misbehave.
[/list]

[h2]Reporting problems[/h2]
Please include a screenshot of the panel and the output log: [i]Cities_Skylines\Cities_Data\output_log.txt[/i] (lines with "RealisticRoads" or "Exception").

Source code (MIT License): https://github.com/Raagulbharatwaj/realistic-roads
```
