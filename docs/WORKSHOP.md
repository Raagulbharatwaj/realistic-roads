## Description (Steam formatting)

```
[h1]Realistic Roads (BETA)[/h1]
CS2-style road drawing for Cities: Skylines. Draw a whole road in one go, see it planned from the side, and build it, with bridges, flyovers, overpasses, underpasses and tunnels at every crossing.

[b]This is a beta.[/b] Save your city before building with it, and please report problems (see below).

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Pick a road, rail or metro track in the game's menus, then click the [b]RR[/b] button (or press [b]Ctrl+Shift+C[/b]).
[*]Click to place points along the route; [b]Tab[/b] switches between curved and straight pieces. Double-click or press [b]Enter[/b] when done.
[*]Check the plan in the side panel: every crossing is listed with its choices (junction, bridge, overpass, underpass, tunnel).
[*]Press [b]Enter[/b] or the check mark to build. [b]Ctrl+Z[/b] in the tool undoes the whole road.
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Long, smooth curves with few nodes; straight pieces with rounded corners, angle and length snapping, typed lengths and turns.
[*]Every crossing planned for you: level junctions, bridges and flyovers (9 m clear), overpasses that lower the other road, underpasses and tunnels. Realistic grades: 4 % for roads, gentle slopes for lowered rail.
[*]Bridges over water and ship routes (20 m clear), or tunnels under the water bed.
[*]Decks: build a road on top of another road (double decker), with columns on the median or frames across it; or one level underground for metro under a street.
[*]Side view: the road against the ground; drag heights, or make a stretch (or the whole road) Elevated or Tunnel.
[*]Planning stage: drag the points before building; everything is shown in the panel before anything is built.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] (subscribe to it).
[/list]

[h2]Known limits (beta)[/h2]
[list]
[*]A road that lowers a rail line very close to a junction may not fit at the rail's gentle grade: cross further from the junction.
[*]Buildings in the way are not removed: bulldoze them first (the panel warns you).
[*]Not tested with every road and track mod; please report the ones that misbehave.
[/list]

[h2]Reporting problems[/h2]
Please include the output log: [i]Cities_Skylines\Cities_Data\output_log.txt[/i] (lines with "RealisticRoads" or "Exception"), and a screenshot of the panel.

Source code (MIT License): https://github.com/Raagulbharatwaj/realistic-roads
```
